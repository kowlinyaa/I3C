• JOURNEY

Every project moves
through three stages.

Every project at I3C progresses through three stages: Idea,
Innovation, and Incubation. The club supports its members
in transforming promising ideas into real-world projects through
this structured process.

STAGE 01

Idea

Every project begins with an idea to solve a problem. Discussion with the relevant team
lead provides guidance, refinement, and
support for practical development.

STAGE 02

Innovation

Ideas are shaped into working concepts through creativity, research, and testing. Team leads
guide refinement, encouraging collaboration toward a practical solution.

STAGE 03

Incubation

Refined projects receive mentorship, resources, and structured support to strengthen implementation. Teams focus on testing and preparing the solution for real-world use.
